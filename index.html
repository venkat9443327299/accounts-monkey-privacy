Accounts Monkey – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

1. Introduction

Accounts Monkey (“Accounts Monkey”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a business billing and management application designed to help businesses manage customers, products, bills, receipts and customer ledger information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through Accounts Monkey, how it is used, how it may be shared with service providers, how it is protected, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and authentication information

If you sign in using phone-number authentication, we may collect and process your mobile phone number and authentication information provided during sign-in. Verification codes are used to verify your identity and secure access to your account.

2.2 Business information entered by you

You may enter information needed to use the application, such as:

  • Business or shop information
  • Customer names and contact details
  • Product and item information
  • Sales bills and invoice information
  • Receipt and payment-related records entered into the app
  • Customer ledger and transaction information
  • Other information that you voluntarily enter into the application

The information you enter should be limited to what is necessary for your business operations.

2.3 Device and technical information

Depending on the services and SDKs enabled in the installed version, technical information may be processed by our service providers, such as device information, app version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, diagnostic information, and information about interactions with the app or advertisements.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain Accounts Monkey.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Store and synchronize business records.
  • Create and manage bills, receipts and customer ledger records.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and detect abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
  • Display advertisements in the free version of the app.

4. Cloud Storage and Firebase Services

Accounts Monkey may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and cloud database services, to provide account authentication and cloud data storage.

Information processed through these services is handled according to the applicable Google service terms and privacy practices. We use reasonable security controls and Firebase security rules appropriate to the application.

5. Advertising

The free version of Accounts Monkey may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, advertisement interactions and related technical information for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement and service operation, subject to applicable Google policies and user settings.

If a premium version or ad-removal option is introduced, the applicable purchase or subscription information may also be processed by the relevant app-store billing service.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or allow access to information with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Google Firebase for authentication and cloud services.
  • Google AdMob for advertising and related measurement services.
  • Other technical service providers only when required to operate, secure, maintain or improve the application.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety or security of users, the service, or others.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information handled through Accounts Monkey. Data transmitted to our cloud services is protected using industry-standard transport security such as HTTPS/TLS where supported by the service.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and authentication information.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and business information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and for legitimate business, security, legal or regulatory purposes.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated account and business data will be deleted, except for information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain. Any retained information will be kept only for the applicable retention period.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Important: Accounts Monkey provides an account-deletion request process. Users may request deletion of their account and associated data using the contact method below.

To request deletion, contact us using the privacy contact shown below and include the phone number or account identifier associated with your Accounts Monkey account. We may request reasonable information to verify the request before deleting the account.

Deletion requests will remove the account and associated application data that we control, subject to legitimate legal, security or regulatory retention requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Accounts Monkey is a business application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing a child-directed service. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Third-Party Services

Accounts Monkey may rely on third-party services such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the applicable privacy information provided by those service providers for additional details about their processing practices.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services or applicable legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this same location with a revised effective date.
