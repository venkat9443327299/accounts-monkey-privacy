Accounts Monkey – Delete Account

This page explains how Accounts Monkey users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Accounts Monkey account, email:

weare33999@gmail.com

Use the subject line Accounts Monkey – Delete My Account and include the phone number associated with your Accounts Monkey account.

What will be deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the account and associated account/business data stored for the service, including customer, product, bill, receipt and ledger data associated with that account, where applicable.

Data that may be retained

We may retain limited information where required by law, for legal claims, fraud prevention, security, or legitimate record-keeping requirements. Such information will be retained only for the period necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing time

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period and will communicate with you if additional information is needed to verify ownership of the account.

Important

Deleting your account may permanently remove your business data from Accounts Monkey. Please keep any records you need before requesting deletion.

Contact

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact weare33999@gmail.com.
